# 0090-horizon-scan — research plan

**Status**: draft

## Goal

Map the tooling territory tracks 0010–0070 did not cover and decide, per area, one of
three calls: **mint a future track** (a full survey is warranted), **adopt directly**
(small enough to recommend from this scan alone), or **skip**. This is a discovery
session, not six full surveys — each area is scored at the depth its call requires, and
the report says so plainly. The area list is the spec's seed (dead code, dependency
architecture, MSW mocking, type coverage) plus what the program itself surfaced: React
Compiler adoption (0040's Key question 4 ruled it onto this track) and bundle-budget
enforcement (intake 2026-08-14 item f raised the budget question; nothing enforces one).
A bounded "anything else" sweep may add areas the seed list missed; it proposes, the
report disposes.

## Key questions

1. **Dead code and unused dependencies** — does knip cover dead exports, unused files,
   and unused dependencies for a TS React app in one tool, and does anything beat it as
   of 2026-08? What does its config cost look like against this stack?
2. **Dependency-architecture rules** — which tool enforces module-boundary rules in CI:
   dependency-cruiser, madge, or an ESLint-lane plugin (boundaries, sheriff)? Can it
   enforce the program's own choke-point invariant (0060: no code path consumes a
   contracted message around the `transport-boundary`) more robustly than the
   oxlint `no-restricted-imports` config the spikes used (D-0020 noted its override
   fragility)?
3. **MSW** — does MSW fit as the test-lane network mock for both legs: REST handlers
   generated by 0010's pipeline tooling, and MQTT-over-WSS via MSW 2.x WebSocket
   interception vs the real-broker (aedes over ws) harness the 0060 spike proved?
4. **Type coverage** — what measures and ratchets `any` leakage (type-coverage,
   typescript-strict-plugin, tsc-strict ratchets), and is the leading tool alive as of
   2026-08?
5. **React Compiler** — what is the adoption path for this app (build-tool fact
   unfilled), what does it obsolete in the adopted 0040 lint lanes, and is the call
   mint-a-track, adopt, or wait?
6. **Bundle-budget enforcement** — what enforces a size budget in CI (size-limit,
   bundlemon), given Wave 2 added ~17 kB gz (TanStack Query) against an unknown budget
   (intake item f)?
7. **Anything else** — does the app profile expose a tooling category the program has
   not touched and the seed list missed? At most three proposals, each with one
   sentence of justification; anything weaker is dropped here, not in the report.
8. **Disposition** — for every area: future track, direct adopt, or skip, with the
   rationale and the risk of deciding at scan depth.

## Candidates

- knip — https://github.com/webpro-nl/knip — dead exports, unused files, unused deps
- dependency-cruiser — https://github.com/sverweij/dependency-cruiser — rules engine over the import graph
- madge — https://github.com/pahen/madge — circular-dependency detection and graphing
- eslint-plugin-boundaries — https://github.com/javierbrea/eslint-plugin-boundaries — ESLint-lane architecture rules
- sheriff — https://github.com/softarc-consulting/sheriff — module-boundary + dependency rules for TS
- msw — https://github.com/mswjs/msw — network-level mocking (REST + WebSocket interception)
- type-coverage — https://github.com/plantain-00/type-coverage — percentage-of-typed-identifiers metric with ratchet
- typescript-strict-plugin — https://github.com/allegro/typescript-strict-plugin — per-file strict-mode ratchet
- babel-plugin-react-compiler — https://github.com/facebook/react — auto-memoization compiler (0040 handoff)
- size-limit — https://github.com/ai/size-limit — CI bundle-budget enforcement
- bundlemon — https://github.com/LironEr/bundlemon — bundle tracking with CI reporting

## Rubric weights

Weights: high / medium / low / n-a. In the report, score each non-n-a criterion
strong / adequate / weak with a sentence of evidence (spec: "Shared evaluation rubric").
Scan depth: areas dispositioned "future track" get their full scoring deferred to that
track; the report scores what the disposition call itself rests on.
