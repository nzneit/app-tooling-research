## a — build tool (and bundler major version), plus monorepo-or-single-package layout

Which build tool does the app use — Vite (which major, and which `@vitejs/plugin-react`
major), webpack, rspack, or other? And is the repo a monorepo or a single package? The
scan assumed Vite throughout. This fact is load-bearing twice: in the bundle-budget
area it eliminated rsdoctor (webpack/rspack-only; it re-enters as size-limit's leading
competitor if the answer is webpack/rspack), and it selects the React Compiler install
recipe outright (Vite 8 / plugin-react v6 needs `@rolldown/plugin-babel` +
`reactCompilerPreset`; earlier Vite uses the legacy `babel:{}` option; webpack/rspack
route through `babel-loader`/`react-compiler-webpack`/`builtin:swc-loader` instead —
at least two distinct non-Vite paths). Layout independently decides whether
knip/type-coverage/size-limit need one config or a per-workspace config array.

reroutes the 0090 bundle-budget and React Compiler dispositions

## b — TypeScript strictness posture

Is the app already on `strict` (or close), and are its remaining `any`s diffuse or
clustered in a few legacy files? Roughly: what does `npx type-coverage --detail` report
in default and `--strict` modes? The scan assumed a modern, already-strict-ish codebase,
and that assumption drove the choice of a percentage ratchet (type-coverage
`--at-least`/`--update-if-higher`) over a per-file strict-migration convention. The
0090 report's type-coverage section carries the decision rule: a high, diffuse baseline
confirms the ratchet as designed; a low or clustered baseline means a per-file
allowlist convention comes first, or the ratchet will be un-movable or gameable.
